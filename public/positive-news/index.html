Positive News without doomscrolling

Positive news helps balance the daily news cycle with stories about progress, kindness, science, health, nature, innovation and community action.

This page collects source-linked positive news from around the world, so you can stay informed without spending all your time in negative headlines.

Use it as a daily place for good news, uplifting stories and constructive current events from multiple countries and topics.

This page is made for readers who want a calmer way to stay informed. It focuses on progress, people, health, science, nature, innovation, communities and meaningful change while keeping links back to original reporting and credible sources.

What you can expect

  • Positive news and good news stories selected for constructive value.
  • Uplifting stories about people, communities, health, science, nature and innovation.
  • Source-linked summaries that make it easier to read more without losing context.
  • A calmer way to follow current events when mainstream feeds feel too negative.

How BrightNews filters stories

BrightNews gathers public news from APIs, RSS feeds and selected source lists, removes duplicates, applies topic filters, and uses AI-assisted review to reduce political, violent, tragic or outrage-driven stories. The goal is not to ignore reality, but to create a more balanced place for constructive and uplifting news.

Frequently asked questions

What is Positive News?

Positive News is a BrightNews roundup focused on positive news, good news, uplifting stories and constructive current events from credible source-linked articles.

How does BrightNews choose positive news?

BrightNews gathers stories from public APIs, RSS feeds and selected source lists, removes duplicates, applies topic filters and uses AI-assisted review to reduce strongly negative, violent, tragic, political or outrage-driven stories.

Does BrightNews replace mainstream news?

No. BrightNews is meant to add balance by making constructive and uplifting stories easier to find when mainstream feeds feel too negative.
