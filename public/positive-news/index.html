Positive news from around the world

Find positive news and uplifting stories with BrightNews. Explore progress in science, health, nature and communities, with links to original reporting.

A balanced view of the world includes the things that are getting better. BrightNews brings together constructive reporting from multiple publishers so you can discover good news without searching through an entire news feed.

Good news worth following

  • Science and health: discoveries, treatments and research that can improve lives.
  • Nature and the planet: conservation, restoration and practical environmental solutions.
  • People and communities: kindness, local initiatives and achievements that help others.
  • Technology and innovation: useful ideas and tools that address real problems.

How we select stories

BrightNews collects articles from RSS feeds and news APIs, removes duplicates and uses topic filters and AI-assisted review to identify constructive stories. This process can make mistakes. Every summary links to the original reporting so you can check the details and context.

Positive news does not mean ignoring difficult events or claiming that every problem has been solved. Our aim is to add perspective with examples of progress, not replace wider news coverage.

Questions about BrightNews

Is BrightNews the original publisher?

No. BrightNews is an independent news aggregator. Publishers remain the sources of the reporting; follow each story's source link to read the full article.

Are all articles translated?

No. The app interface is available in several languages, but articles and summaries may remain in their source language. Use the language, country and topic filters to find relevant stories.

Can I read on my phone?

Yes. BrightNews works in a web browser and is also available as an Android app. You can browse stories and save them to return to later.
